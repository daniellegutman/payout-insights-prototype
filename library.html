Design system · insights

One card per rule type, per format.

Six rule types. Two render in a fixed format; four render in dollars, units, or percentages. Every card shows the headline number, the change vs prior period, and an employee ranking.

Aggregation: Commission shows the team aggregate. Every other rule type shows the team average.

Format constraints: Commission is always currency. Rating is always a number. Prorated renders the same as All-or-Nothing.
